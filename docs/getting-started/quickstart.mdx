# Quickstart

This walkthrough takes you from a fresh CDT account to a working digital twin in roughly 30 minutes: exploring the platform on a map, adding a building, uploading an IFC model, and inviting a teammate.

## Before you start

You need an account on a CDT instance, with the URL from your administrator, and a modern browser with WebGL enabled. A sample IFC file is useful for step 4 — public test files are at the [buildingSMART sample repository](https://github.com/buildingSMART/Sample-Test-Files).

No account yet? Your organization's administrator can invite you, or you can create an instance using the [Installation guide](./installation.mdx).

## Step 1 — Sign in (≈ 2 minutes)

1. Open your CDT URL in a browser.
2. Sign in with your email and password, or with Google if your administrator has enabled OAuth.
3. Enter the MFA code sent by email or authenticator app. Cookies skip this on later visits from the same browser.
4. CDT opens to the **Map Viewer**, centred on your organization's default view.

You should see a map filling the viewport, a left sidebar of navigation icons, and a top toolbar with search and tools.

## Step 2 — Explore the map (≈ 5 minutes)

1. **Pan and zoom** with mouse drag and scroll wheel. Hold right-click and drag to tilt and rotate in 3D.
2. **Search a location** by typing a Canadian address or place name in the top-left search bar.
3. **Toggle a data layer** by clicking the **Datasets** icon in the bottom toolbar and adding one, for example "Statistics Canada — Census tracts". It appears on the map immediately.
4. **Inspect a feature** by clicking any building footprint or layer feature to see its attributes in a side panel.

See the [Map Viewer guide](../guides/map-viewer.md) for more.

## Step 3 — Add a building (≈ 5 minutes)

1. Open the **Buildings** view from the left sidebar.
2. Click **Add Building**.
3. Fill in a **Name** (for example *Test Building 1*), a **Type** from the dropdown, and a **Location** by dropping a pin on the map or pasting latitude/longitude.
4. Click **Save**.

The building appears in the buildings list and as a marker on the map. Clicking it opens the **Building Details** panel.

See [Buildings & Sites](../guides/buildings-and-sites.md) for full coverage.

## Step 4 — Upload an IFC model (≈ 8 minutes)

1. Open the building you just created.
2. Switch to the **Map** or **BIM** viewer.
3. Use the **Add tool** in the toolbar to drop your IFC onto the panel, or click **Upload** and select it. Files under 50 MB finish in under a minute; larger ones take longer. The File section in the app sidebar also manages uploads and shows progress.
4. Once processing completes, switch to the **BIM Viewer** from the left sidebar and select your file.
5. Orbit with **left-click + drag**, pan with **right-click + drag**, and zoom with the scroll wheel. Click any element to see its IFC property sets in the right panel.

The platform converts the file to Fragments on upload, so subsequent loads are fast.

See the [BIM Viewer guide](../guides/bim-viewer.md) for more.

## Step 5 — Add a teammate (≈ 5 minutes)

This step requires the **Admin** role.

1. Open **Settings → Users** from the left sidebar.
2. Click **Add User**.
3. In the **Create New User** dialog, fill in **Name**, **Email**, **Role** (Viewer / User / Admin), and a **Temporary Password** to share with them.
4. Click **Create User**.

The teammate appears in the users list and can sign in with the temporary password, scoped to their role's permissions.

See [Managing roles and permissions](../authorization/managing-roles.mdx) for the full role reference.

## Verify everything works

You should now be able to sign in and reach the Map Viewer, pan and search the map, toggle an open-data layer and click features for attributes, see your new building in the list and on the map, open your IFC model in the BIM viewer and inspect element properties, and see your invited teammate in the members list.

If any step did not work, see [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.mdx).
